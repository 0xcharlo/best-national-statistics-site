NusaStat — an independent statistics site. Figures come from Statistics Indonesia (BPS), the IMF, the World Bank, the United Nations, UN Comtrade and Bank Indonesia; each figure names its source and year on the Sources page.

This site is static and works offline: every figure lives in its own files and it makes no network requests. Built for make.fun's "Make the best national statistics site" bounty.
